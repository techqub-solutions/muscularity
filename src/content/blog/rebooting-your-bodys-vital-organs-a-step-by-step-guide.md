Poor diet, stress and too little exercise all take a toll on your organs. If you feel sluggish, tired or out of balance, it may be time to give your body's vital systems a reboot. Here's how to care for each major organ.

## 1. Liver: your detox powerhouse

Your liver filters toxins and helps with digestion. Poor diet, alcohol and even medication can overwork it.

- **Eat antioxidant-rich foods:** berries, leafy greens and vegetables like broccoli support your liver.
- **Stay hydrated:** water helps flush out toxins.
- **Limit alcohol and processed foods:** they overload your liver.

## 2. Gut: the engine of digestion

Your gut breaks down food and absorbs nutrients. Poor gut health can cause fatigue, bloating and brain fog.

- **Add probiotics:** yogurt, kimchi and kombucha support healthy gut bacteria.
- **Eat more fibre:** fruit, vegetables and whole grains keep digestion regular.
- **Eat mindfully:** chew slowly and avoid overeating.

## 3. Heart: the lifelong pump

A sedentary lifestyle and poor diet can lead to heart disease.

- **Exercise regularly:** aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise a week, such as walking, swimming or cycling.
- **Eat heart-healthy fats:** omega-3s from fish, flaxseeds and walnuts.
- **Manage stress:** meditation, yoga and breathing exercises all help.

## 4. Lungs: the breath of life

Pollution, smoking and inactivity can all reduce lung function.

- **Practise deep breathing** to improve lung capacity.
- **Do cardio:** running and swimming help expand lung capacity and improve circulation.
- **Avoid pollutants:** limit exposure to smoke, chemicals and allergens.

## 5. Kidneys: your filtration system

Your kidneys filter waste and excess water from your blood.

- **Stay hydrated** so your kidneys can work efficiently.
- **Limit salt:** too much raises blood pressure and stresses your kidneys.
- **Eat potassium-rich foods:** bananas, spinach and avocados.

## 6. Skin: your largest organ

Your skin protects you and regulates temperature, but it faces sun, toxins and dehydration.

- **Hydrate from within:** water keeps skin cells plump.
- **Eat your vitamins:** vitamin C, E and beta-carotene from fruit and vegetables.
- **Wear sunscreen daily.**

## Rest and mental wellness

Your organs work together. Good sleep, regular rest and stress management give your body the chance to repair itself. Mindfulness and short breaks during the day help reset both mind and body.

## Final thoughts

A body reboot doesn't need to be complicated. Small, sustainable changes add up to better health now and long-term wellness later. Listen to your body: it's the only one you have.
