Cardio (cardiovascular or aerobic training) is essential for fitness and health. It raises your heart rate, improves lung capacity and builds endurance and stamina. Whether you're aiming for fat loss, a healthier heart or simply more energy, cardio plays a central role.

## Why cardio is important

- **Heart health:** cardio strengthens your heart, so it pumps blood and delivers oxygen more efficiently. That lowers the risk of heart disease, lowers blood pressure and improves cholesterol levels.
- **Fat burning and weight loss:** from a brisk walk to high-intensity interval training (HIIT), cardio burns calories and boosts your metabolism.
- **Mental health:** cardio releases endorphins, your body's natural mood lifters. Regular cardio helps relieve stress and can reduce symptoms of anxiety and depression.
- **Endurance and stamina:** climbing stairs, playing with your kids and playing sport all get easier as your stamina grows.

## Types of cardio workouts

The best cardio is the one that suits your preferences and fitness level:

- **Running and jogging:** simple, accessible and possible almost anywhere. Builds endurance and strengthens your legs and core.
- **Cycling:** low-impact and great for your joints, on a stationary bike or outdoors.
- **High-intensity interval training (HIIT):** short bursts of hard work with brief rests. Maximum benefit in less time.
- **Swimming:** a full-body workout that's easy on the joints and great for lung capacity.
- **Jump rope:** often overlooked, but excellent for your heart rate and coordination.
- **Dance workouts:** Zumba or aerobics, a fun way to stay active to music.

## How often should you do cardio?

- **For general health:** at least 150 minutes of moderate cardio a week (such as brisk walking), or 75 minutes of vigorous cardio (such as running).
- **For weight loss:** you may need more, with more vigorous sessions or HIIT.
- **For endurance:** combine longer steady sessions with interval training.

## Combine cardio with strength

For a balanced routine, pair cardio with strength training. Cardio burns fat and builds endurance, while strength training builds muscle, which raises your metabolism even further. We encourage every client to combine both.

## Final thoughts

Cardio isn't just about weight loss. It builds a strong, resilient heart, gives you more energy and makes you feel better, physically and mentally. That's why it's part of every programme we write, at home, outdoors or in the gym.
