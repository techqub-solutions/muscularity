Our general fitness programmes suit every level, from complete beginners to seasoned athletes. Whether your goal is weight loss, toning or simply more energy, your plan gives you structure, motivation and a coach who keeps you on track.

## What you'll do

- **Cardio:** circuit training, running, cycling and aerobic work to strengthen your heart and burn calories.
- **Strength and toning:** resistance and bodyweight training to build lean muscle.
- **Flexibility and mobility:** stretching and dynamic warm-ups to move better and prevent injury.
- **HIIT or low-impact:** the right intensity for you, from high-intensity intervals to gentle sessions.

## What you get

- **One-to-one guidance** on form, motivation and your goals.
- **More energy** and stamina for everyday life.
- **Weight management** through the right balance of cardio and strength.
- **A calmer mind:** regular exercise helps reduce stress.
- **Regular progress checks** to celebrate milestones and keep improving.
