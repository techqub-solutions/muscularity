Cross Fit mixes strength, conditioning and bodyweight skills into short, intense workouts. With a personal trainer by your side, every movement is coached, every workout is scaled to you, and every session moves you forward.

## What you'll do

- **Strength lifts:** squats, deadlifts and presses to build a solid base of strength.
- **Bodyweight skills:** push-ups, pull-ups, lunges and core work that build control and power.
- **Conditioning:** interval circuits that raise your heart rate and push your endurance.
- **Coached technique:** clear cues on every rep, so you move safely as the intensity rises.

## What you get

- **Strength and stamina together:** one session trains both.
- **A plan that changes:** workouts vary week to week, so you keep progressing.
- **Progress you can see:** we track your lifts and times and adjust your plan as you improve.
