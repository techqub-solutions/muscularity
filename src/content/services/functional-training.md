Functional training builds strength and coordination through exercises that mirror everyday movement. Instead of working one muscle at a time, you train several together, so daily tasks feel easier, steadier and safer.

## What you'll do

- **Real-life movement patterns:** squatting, lunging, bending, lifting and pushing, from carrying shopping to climbing stairs.
- **Multi-muscle exercises:** kettlebell swings, deadlifts, push presses and medicine ball throws.
- **Core strength and stability:** planks, rotations and stability ball work for posture and control.

## What you get

- **Easier daily life:** strength and agility for lifting a child, moving furniture or playing sport.
- **Injury protection:** stronger stabilising muscles, ligaments and joints.
- **Better balance and coordination:** more fluid, precise movement.
- **A programme built for your body,** for performance or simply moving better.
