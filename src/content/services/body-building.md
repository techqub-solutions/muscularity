Bodybuilding at Muscularity Fitness is built for one thing: helping you add muscle, sharpen definition and get stronger. Your programme blends proven hypertrophy principles with a personal plan and a coach who keeps you accountable.

## What you'll do

- **A custom workout plan** for your goal, whether that's bulking up, cutting fat or preparing for a competition.
- **Hypertrophy training:** progressive overload, drop sets, supersets and high-volume work for balanced growth.
- **Compound lifts:** squats, deadlifts and bench presses to build mass and a strong foundation.
- **Isolation work:** curls, triceps extensions and leg presses to fine-tune each muscle group.
- **Core stability:** targeted core work for a strong, balanced physique.

## What you get

- **Nutrition guidance** to fuel muscle growth, recovery and fat loss.
- **Competition prep** with posing practice, stage presence coaching and detailed meal plans.
- **Progress tracking:** strength assessments and body composition checks, so your plan keeps adjusting.
- **Realistic goals** and the accountability to reach them.
