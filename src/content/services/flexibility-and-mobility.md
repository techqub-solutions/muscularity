Flexibility and mobility sessions increase your range of motion, release muscle tightness and support healthy joints. Whether you want to lift better, run better or simply move comfortably through the day, this is the foundation.

## What you'll do

- **Stretching techniques:** static and dynamic stretches that reduce stiffness and improve muscle elasticity.
- **Mobility drills:** lunges, hip openers and shoulder rotations that open up your joints while keeping them strong.
- **Yoga-inspired movement:** flowing sequences that lengthen muscles, release tension and improve balance.

## What you get

- **Fewer injuries:** flexible joints and elastic muscles handle stress and impact better.
- **Better performance:** better movement patterns in lifting, running and sport.
- **Faster recovery:** more blood flow to your muscles and less soreness after training.
- **Everyday comfort:** better posture and fewer aches and pains.
