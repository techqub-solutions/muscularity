Muscularity Fitness is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

## What we collect

When you book a session or send us a message, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if you give it), where and when you want to train, your goal, and any notes you add. Our forms open WhatsApp with these details filled in, so the information reaches us only when you choose to send it.

If you apply for a job, we receive the CV and details you email to us.

## How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, arrange and deliver your training, and provide customer service. With your permission, we may also tell you about offers.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties unless the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email us at [info@muscularityfitness.com](mailto:info@muscularityfitness.com).

## Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

## Health information

If you tell us about an injury or medical condition, we use it only to plan safe training for you.
