## 1. Eligibility

Clients must be at least 18 years old, or accompanied by a legal guardian if younger. Everyone completes a health and fitness waiver before their first session, to confirm they are cleared for exercise and understand the risks of physical activity.

## 2. Payments and fees

Personal training and packages are paid in full before services begin. We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer and online payment. Promotions and discounts are non-refundable and cannot be combined with other offers. Report any billing questions to us within 30 days. Late payment may pause your sessions.

## 3. Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel or reschedule a session with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, the session is charged in full. Where a refund applies, we process it within 14 business days. In case of serious illness or injury, we can pause or extend your package with proper documentation.

## 4. Code of conduct

We expect clients to treat trainers and staff with respect and courtesy. Harassment, abusive language or disruptive behaviour may lead to suspension or termination of services without a refund.

## 5. Timekeeping

Please be ready 5–10 minutes before your session. Sessions start on time, and trainers are not obliged to extend a session for late arrivals. If your trainer is more than 10 minutes late, you receive an extra session free of charge.

## 6. What to wear

Wear comfortable workout clothes and athletic shoes (no sandals or open-toe shoes). For safety, avoid jewellery or accessories that could get in the way of exercise.

## 7. Personal belongings

Muscularity Fitness is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged personal items. Please bring only what you need.

## 8. Health and safety

Tell your trainer about any medical condition, recent injury or discomfort before and during training. We recommend a fitness assessment before starting any new programme. Muscularity Fitness is not responsible for injuries from unsupervised exercise or equipment use.

## 9. Equipment

Use equipment as instructed and follow your trainer's safety guidance at all times. Damage caused by misuse may be charged to the person responsible.
